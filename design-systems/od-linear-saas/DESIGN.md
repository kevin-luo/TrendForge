# od-linear-saas

## palette
- background: `#F7F9FC`
- surface: `#FFFFFF`
- ink: `#0F172A`
- muted: `#64748B`
- accents: `#2563EB`, `#14B8A6`, `#0EA5E9`

## typography
- display: Inter Display
- body: Inter
- mono: IBM Plex Mono
- headline weight: 900
- body weight: 700

## spacing
- Use an 8px grid.
- Keep panel padding at 24 to 40 px.
- Keep label spacing tight and deliberate.

## layout
- Use a clean dashboard grid with one primary hero block.
- Keep cards aligned to clear columns.
- Keep the top row light and decisive.

## components
- Product cards
- Metric chips
- Inline labels
- Small dashboard panes

## motion
- Fade and slide in with short timing.
- Pulse active metrics with subtle scale.
- Transition between panels with structured movement.

## voice
- Direct, practical, and product-led.
- Short labels carry the meaning.
- Every line should help the user scan faster.

## brand
- Calm SaaS clarity.
- Modern, precise, and trustworthy.
- The interface should feel built, not decorated.

## anti-patterns
- Heavy texture
- Decorative clutter
- Loose alignment
- Long label chains
- Overeager motion
