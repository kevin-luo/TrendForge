## spacing
- Use compact technical spacing.
- Keep panel edges crisp.
- Leave enough room for signal lines and labels.

## layout
- Build a telemetry canvas with clear panes.
- Keep the central signal readable at a glance.
- Use precise alignment across dashboards and rails.

## components
- Signal panes
- Telemetry labels
- Data rails
- Neon accent chips

## motion
- Use scan, tick, and orbit motion.
- Pulse active states with neon energy.
- Keep transitions quick and technical.

## voice
- Technical, direct, and concise.
- Use short labels and sharp claims.
- Keep the tone like a live system readout.

## brand
- Dark systems intelligence.
- High signal density with control.
- Crisp, live, and engineered.

## anti-patterns
- Soft pastel styling
- Weak contrast
- Fuzzy labels
- Random glow
- Loose grid alignment
