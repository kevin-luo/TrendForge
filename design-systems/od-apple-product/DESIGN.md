# od-apple-product

## palette
- background: `#F5F5F7`
- surface: `#FFFFFF`
- ink: `#1D1D1F`
- muted: `#6E6E73`
- accents: `#0071E3`, `#A1A1AA`, `#C7CBD1`

## typography
- display: Inter Display
- body: Inter
- mono: IBM Plex Mono
- headline weight: 900
- body weight: 650

## spacing
- Use wide breathing room.
- Keep spacing even and almost invisible.
- Let product silhouette own the stage.

## layout
- Center the hero product.
- Keep supporting notes low and quiet.
- Use balanced symmetry with soft offset.

## components
- Device hero blocks
- Fine feature notes
- Soft shadow cards
- Minimal CTAs

## motion
- Use smooth reveals and measured scale.
- Keep camera drift subtle.
- Give the product room to settle before text arrives.

## voice
- Polished, calm, and exact.
- Use fewer words with stronger nouns.
- Keep claims restrained and confident.

## brand
- Premium hardware confidence.
- Clean material surfaces.
- Precise and quietly luxurious.

## anti-patterns
- Loud borders
- Heavy gradients
- Dense copy blocks
- Jagged spacing
- Aggressive transitions
