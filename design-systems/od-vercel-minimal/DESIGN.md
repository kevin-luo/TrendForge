# od-vercel-minimal

## palette
- background: `#FAFAF9`
- surface: `#FFFFFF`
- ink: `#111111`
- muted: `#6B7280`
- accents: `#111111`, `#6366F1`, `#22C55E`

## typography
- display: Inter
- body: Inter
- mono: IBM Plex Mono
- headline weight: 900
- body weight: 650

## spacing
- Use generous whitespace and a strict grid.
- Keep gaps large enough to breathe.
- Keep the composition sparse and intentional.

## layout
- Place one strong focal block in each frame.
- Keep supporting content in clean side rails.
- Use symmetry with small asymmetries for tension.

## components
- Minimal cards
- Thin dividers
- Small badges
- Quiet callouts

## motion
- Use gentle fades and measured easing.
- Reveal content with short vertical drift.
- Keep motion reserved for emphasis.

## voice
- Calm, precise, and concise.
- Use simple language and compact claims.
- Let hierarchy carry the story.

## brand
- Sharp minimalism.
- High trust and low noise.
- Confident restraint.

## anti-patterns
- Dense panels
- Decorative gradients
- Extra outlines
- Loud shadows
- Busy backgrounds
