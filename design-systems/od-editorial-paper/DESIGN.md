# od-editorial-paper

## palette
- background: `#F6F0E2`
- surface: `#FFF9F0`
- ink: `#17131A`
- muted: `#6F6258`
- accents: `#B45309`, `#1E3A8A`, `#9F1239`

## typography
- display: Noto Serif CJK SC
- body: Inter
- mono: IBM Plex Mono
- headline weight: 900
- body weight: 700

## spacing
- Use print-like margins and columns.
- Keep text blocks disciplined.
- Let the grid feel editorial and calm.

## layout
- Build a magazine page rhythm.
- Use pull quotes and evidence blocks.
- Keep the composition column-based.

## components
- Quote panels
- Evidence cards
- Section labels
- Paper texture bands

## motion
- Use page-turn and paper-slide cues.
- Reveal text with soft timing.
- Keep bigger motion for key facts.

## voice
- Editorial, thoughtful, and measured.
- Use language with conviction and restraint.
- Keep the reading pace comfortable.

## brand
- Print culture with modern clarity.
- Warm paper and sharp ink.
- Serious, thoughtful, and cultured.

## anti-patterns
- Plastic gloss
- Neon accents
- Tight column crush
- Loud motion
- Texture that competes with text
